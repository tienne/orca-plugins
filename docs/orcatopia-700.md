# Orcatopia 700-place water expansion

English · [한국어](orcatopia-700.ko.md)

## Scope and counts

This unreleased source change preserves all 600 existing places and adds 50 sea places and 50 river places. It provides **700 normal places, 1,400 normal/+ variants and 153 country groupings**. The final placement counts are **504 land / 72 sea / 91 river / 33 land & sea**. Country labels are collection groupings. The seven additional groupings are Isle of Man, Marshall Islands, Kiribati, Bahamas, Sweden, Guyana and Paraguay. South Korea grows from 14 land places to 19 total: 14 land, three sea lights and two river bridges.

Tiers were allocated based on the existing water catalog and place features, with all four tiers present in each new theme. Rare entries emphasise recognisable engineering or ecological forms; Epic entries include the named arch bridge, major protected marine systems, historic engineering and a major waterfall; Phoenix Islands and Panama Canal form the two Wonder entries. These are collection ranks. Existing 600 ranks stay unchanged. Draw weights remain **70/23/6/1%**. With every theme unlocked, per-place chances are about 0.21021% / 0.07958% / 0.09375% / 0.07143%; a locked collection uses only the unlocked candidates within each tier.

There are **2,139 decorations**: 1,426 themed pieces, five free basics, four focal garden pieces, 700 fusion star monuments and four tourism buildings. Water-only pieces total **392 = 196 × 2**. There are 713 normal first-discovery gift IDs and 700 first-fusion gift IDs. The earlier 13 water-compatible places keep their extra pairs. New sea gifts use coral or navigation buoys according to the scene; river pieces use lotus beds, rafts or waterwheels. Garden designs remain fictional.

## Selection, classification and provenance

Sea candidates include offshore rock/island lights, reefs, lagoons, marine sanctuaries, submerged vessel remains and a bridge across a tidal harbour. River candidates include river bridges, navigable canals and lifts, and in-channel waterfalls or rapids. The river game category includes sea-level shipping canals. Sea/island silhouettes include the surrounding marine water; these are miniature placement conventions.

Identity checks compare all 700 IDs and normalised names and examine aliases and inclusion relationships. Tower Bridge and Tower of London are separate structures; Sydney Harbour Bridge and Sydney Opera House are separate structures. Charles Bridge, Ponte Vecchio and Pulteney Bridge are separate from the existing Prague clock, Florence Cathedral and Roman Baths. The Alcántara candidate is the Tagus bridge in Extremadura, separate from the bridge of the same name in Toledo. Apo Reef is west of Mindoro, separate from Apo Island near Negros. Broad existing places such as Venice, the Great Barrier Reef, Ha Long Bay, Galápagos and Komodo are excluded as sources of nested extra counts; no new entries are counted separately inside the new Bikini, Phoenix, Namena or other marine entries.

The 100 linked institutional references below support names, countries, water connections and the original English/Korean summaries. Sources include lighthouse operators, UNESCO, parks agencies, national/city tourism authorities and the bridge’s architect. `scripts/data/orcatopia-water-100-sources.json` records retrieval date, direct-page text checksum when available, source-access method, coordinates and their separate provenance. On 2026-11-17, **86 pages were retrieved directly and 14 were checked through their indexed official text** because direct access returned errors or bot restrictions. An indexed reference has no fabricated page checksum.

Coordinates are atlas representative points. **39** use official operator/map metadata, UNESCO datasets, Ramsar positions or an official submitted boundary; **61** use separately cited Wikidata, Wikimedia or OpenStreetMap coordinate corroboration. The official New Zealand light descriptions round positions to whole minutes. For marine parks and long canals, a marker can represent a larger area or a named component rather than an exact illustrated reef. Namena uses its island as a representative point for the surrounding reserve. Field checks and precise boundary surveys are outside this verification. Source discrepancies were retained as review notes: a Howrah Wikidata candidate had the wrong longitude sign and was rejected; a generic Gullfoss map search returned a different feature and was replaced with the waterfall object; tourist map centres for Ponte Vecchio and Pulteney were replaced with feature-specific corroboration. None of these sources are requested at runtime.

## Integration and preservation

`catalog-700.json` is the curated source for the new records, Korean translations, water surface, structural proportions and scene composition. `sync-orcatopia-water-700.mjs` synchronises the runtime catalogs after checking the original 600 records and translations against committed hashes. `pnpm nx run landmark-island:regenerate-catalog` runs the earlier expansion waves and this synchronisation as two ordered steps. Direct execution of the existing generator also keeps the new 100 entries. Illustration and scene modules are bundled locally. Placement, collection, map points, filtering, first-discovery gifts and fusion monuments reuse the existing dynamic catalog contracts.

Existing IDs, quantities, placements, gifts, fusions, opened land, save keys and v8 backups stay compatible. Loading the larger catalog grants no landmarks, tickets or materials. River/sea collection thresholds stay at 12/25 different discoveries, and first exploration still consumes one existing ticket per theme. Locked themes remain outside normal draws; every expanded guaranteed-theme pool contains four tiers. Empty-tier rescaling remains covered. Tourism uses distinct base place IDs, keeping duplicate and normal/+ counting, the offline cap, rates and special-building prices unchanged. No tourism expansion or host capability is added.

## Verification and review artifacts

The Nx unit target checks all 700 places, the old 600 record/translation hashes and 1,200 normal/+ artwork hashes and 600 normal scene hashes from the base commit, geometry uniqueness after identity/paint-prefix removal, scene composition uniqueness, normal/+ preservation, placement and decoration surfaces, all gated and guaranteed tier candidates, fusion/gift conservation, migrations, backup/restore and tourism. Browser fixtures cover bilingual details, source links, fusion, 700-card lazy rendering, offscreen animation pause, reduced motion, map markers and data retention. A new SVG path-length check catches paths that have valid strings but cannot draw.

`catalog-700.spec.ts` produces five English **DEMO** JPEG contact sheets (20 entries each), with preview and 40-pixel drawings on sea and river backgrounds, in the ignored test-results folder. These fixtures never use real saves. Existing 600 scene/record content remains unchanged; new 100 variants use the same additive + effects and motion controls. Run `pnpm nx run landmark-island:typecheck`, `:test`, `:build` and `:ui-test`, then validate the built artifact. Generated builds and review screenshots are excluded from development commits. Packaging/CLI validation is local evidence; public-directory registration, execution approval, release and real-user-state injection are outside this change.
